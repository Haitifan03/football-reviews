---
title: Indiana 55 vs Howard 0
date: 2026-09-14
author: Mason Holland

---

Well, the defense looks better, Hoover looks worse, and the worst team in FCS football isn't better than the best college football team in the country last year. This game was important to Curt Cignetti's squad and far from a useless game. They oiled the gears, worked out the kinks, and gained a little confidence, but boy was it a unexciting watch. I have a lot of faith in the scheduling of the Hoosiers, but I can't say it leads to the most exciting start to the season.

I think throughout this season, the connection between Charlie Becker and Josh Hoover will continue to grow and I could see a world where both of them are in attendance for the Heisman ceremony. Becker's ability to turn bad throws into good catches is a aspect of his game that shines more than even his speed or route running ability. He seems to have taken on the Mendoza moniker as the humble weapon that will fight tooth and nail for every catch no matter how unlikely the catch looks, then turn around and spend the first minute of a interview talking about how blessed he is to have the opportunity to step on the field every week.

On Sunday, IU moved up one spot to #4 in the AP Poll. I love the ranking, it's always fun to see the number next to their name grow smaller, but this one was unearned and gained simply by the loses of other teams ahead of them. Last week and this week the Oregon Ducks have continued their fall from grace thanks to Boise State and Oklahoma State, and either Ohio State or Texas had to lose on Saturday night, though I was impressed by the fashion in which OSU chose to do just that. I guess I shouldn't be surprised by a Ohio State kicker missing a high pressure kick in the last minutes of a high profile game, but ouch. That one will hurt for a while. It also means that OSU is going to have a fire lit under their ass during their game at IU on October 17. That's going to be a hell of a fun game to watch.

Next week IU will finish up their non-conference slate against Western Kentucky. Right now they have a 99% chance of winning that game so if you aren't able to catch the game, there shouldn't be too much to worry about. Hopefully this will be a good chance for Nick Marsh to step into a bigger role on the IU receiving core after a disappointing showing against Howard, but part of that will be on Josh Hoover who missed him several times on Saturday. Western Kentucky should put up more of a fight than Howard, though it will still be a fight out of their weight class. I expect the score to be 56-10 in favor of the Hoosiers once 6pm hits next Saturday.
