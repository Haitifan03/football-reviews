---
title: Ole Miss 41 vs Louisville 38
date: 2026-09-05
author: Mason Holland

---

This was the only matchup this week with two top 25 teams and it proved to be
everything it was hyped up to be. Trinidad Chambliss is definitely a Heisman contender, and
maybe one of the most exciting QB’s in football, and Kewan Lacy is a monster of a man. That
isn’t to say this team is perfect however, Louisville hung in there and traded haymakers with
them the entire second half. And during the first half, they did their best to make Trinidad look
like he was going to regress from last year to the tune of 2 interceptions. But what makes a
good QB is one that can recover from mistakes and he did just that.


Louisville will be a interesting team to keep a eye on this year. Jeff Brom’s team the last
two years has been a middle of the pack 8-4 team with hopes for something better, and based
on this performance there might be something there. The ACC doesn’t have a clear number 2
right now behind Miami in my opinion and the Cardinals are just one of several teams with a
keen eye on that spot.
