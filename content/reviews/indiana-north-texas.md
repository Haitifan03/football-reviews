This may be stating the obvious but Indiana isn't the same team that won the National Championship a year ago.
That's not to say they won't be just as good or that they are a bad
team, but it did feel a little sad to watch this game and not see D'Angelo Ponds causing havoc
on defense, or Fernando Mendoza's goofy post game speeches. Thankfully, Indiana still is
coached by the best coach in the NCAA, and Josh Hoover adds a bang to the passing game
that leaves me excited for the season.



North Texas was a good opponent, and their offense highlighted weaknesses in
Indiana’s defense, particularly in the secondary. Now, for context, the top two cornerbacks for IU
were injured in the first quarter of the game, and shouldn’t be out for too long (they are listed
day-to-day). But injuries are a constant in football, and the backups could become a liability
before the season comes to a close. Thankfully Indiana will have two more cupcakes to iron out
all the issues before conference play begins, and 4/5 more cupcakes before a serious challenge
appears depending on whether you consider Nebraska to be a challenge
